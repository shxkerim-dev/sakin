City records and alternate names: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/ Data: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ Original coordinates, time zones and city coverage are retained. Alternate names and population were joined by country/name and coordinates; alternative spellings are normalized for search. Localized display names: GeoNames alternateNamesV2.zip, same CC BY 4.0 licence. Preferred non-historic names for en/ru/az/tr/ar; original name is retained when no translation is available.

MOSQUE-DATA-NOTICES.txt

Mosque locations: OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ Search provided by Photon (https://photon.komoot.io) with Overpass fallback. Photon is a public best-effort service; results are cached and searches run only on opening/changing the selected search area, at most once per hour per area after success. mosques-starter.json is a small, incomplete, offline starter extract for Baku, Moscow and the Sydney/Mosman area, retrieved 2026-10-29 from Photon using area bounds and mosque/local-language search terms. It is not a complete or verified directory of active congregations. Raw responses and the derived JSON are included in the accompanying source archive under the ODbL. Locations and names can be corrected through OpenStreetMap. Opening hours and current prayer access are not verified.
