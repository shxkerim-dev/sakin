About Sakin

Your prayer day

See the next prayer, a live countdown and a progress arc. Swipe the main card through upcoming prayers or browse nearby dates. Prayer calculations work offline with several methods, Hanafi or standard Asr, high-latitude rules, time zones and minute adjustments.

A sky that follows your day

The background responds to the selected location and local time: sunrise/daylight, sunset and night, an illustrative Sun position, a textured Moon with a date-based phase, stars, clouds, rain, snow, fog and wind effects when current model weather is available. Time and weather previews change the visual scene, not prayer calculations.

Find your direction and a mosque

Use the Qibla compass and calibration guide. Search nearby mosques within 2, 5, 10 or 25 km, browse clustered map markers and expand the result list. Tap to select a mosque and preview a glowing route; long-press for directions, saving and sharing. Road previews use an external service and fall back to a labelled straight guide. Near me uses an explicitly requested phone position; city mode uses the selected centre.

Make it comfortable

Choose English, Russian, Azerbaijani, Turkish or Arabic. Search city names in many scripts; display translations follow the interface when available. Use light, dark, system or fixed Graphite appearance. Adjust palette, sky intensity, animation, floating cards and subtle phone tilt, or enable Reduce motion. A compact home screen widget shows the next prayer, countdown, progress and day’s times.

Stay informed

Set reminders at prayer time or 5, 10 or 15 minutes before, with system notification sound or without sound. Review delivery permissions and diagnostic tests. No account, ads or analytics SDK is included. Prayer calculations and city search are offline; weather, map images, live mosque search and road routing require internet. Exact updates and reminders require the relevant Android permissions.
