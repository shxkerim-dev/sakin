Privacy policy

Operator and scope

Sakin is provided by Kerim Shikhkamalov, Azerbaijan. Privacy and support contact: sakin.official.01@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android app, version 1.0. Effective date: 31 October 2026.

What stays on your device

There is no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated tracking backend in this build. City search runs offline. A local notice acknowledgement stores its revision and time and is not uploaded. Settings, selected coordinates and time zone, prayer adjustments, saved mosques, cached weather/map results, and reminder diagnostics are stored locally. Compass and tilt sensor readings are processed on the device and are not uploaded.

Location and permission

You can choose a city or enter coordinates without granting location permission. Device location is requested when you choose Current location or Near me; approximate or precise foreground location is used as allowed by Android. No background-location permission is requested. Selected GPS coordinates can be saved for prayer calculations; nearby-search and HTTP caches may also contain location coordinates. Permission can be revoked in Android settings.

When data leaves the device

Weather sends selected coordinates to Open-Meteo. Mosque queries send a centre, radius and search terms to Photon or Overpass. OpenStreetMap receives tile coordinates, which can reveal the viewed area. Selecting a mosque sends map-origin and destination coordinates to OSRM for a route preview. If enabled, secure DNS fallback sends service hostnames to Cloudflare or Google after system DNS fails. These services also receive network information such as IP address, request time and HTTP headers.

External actions and choices

Google Maps or another maps app opens only after an explicit action and receives the destination or search query. Sharing opens the Android chooser with a mosque name and coordinates; the chosen app applies its own policy. Weather background and secure DNS fallback can be disabled. Route requests occur on selection, not as background tracking. Using a manually selected city still involves network requests for the optional online features.

Purpose and security

Processing is used to calculate/display prayer times, show weather, discover mosques, render maps and preview routes. Requests use HTTPS with certificate validation. Local storage is protected by the Android app sandbox; this is not a promise of end-to-end encryption. No sale of personal data is implemented. Third-party providers operate independently; their logging, retention and international processing are governed by their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Selected settings and saved places remain until replaced or cleared. Reminder diagnostics record local delivery status and are overwritten by later attempts. HTTP caches are bounded and can be evicted; some saved place caches remain until replaced or app data is cleared. Clear Sakin storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove app-held local data. App backup is disabled, but platform/vendor processes are outside our control. We cannot erase independent provider logs; contact the relevant provider or sakin.official.01@gmail.com about applicable rights and requests.

Children, changes and contact

Sakin is a general-purpose prayer utility, not a child-directed social service, and contains no account-based profiling. The publisher must select the correct Play target audience. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion or exercise other statutory rights through sakin.official.01@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be published with a new date/revision. Mandatory local rights remain unaffected.
